What is BigQuery -
Google BigQuery is a fully managed, serverless, enterprise cloud data warehouse in Google Cloud Platform (GCP). 
It lets you store, manage, and query massive datasets—ranging from gigabytes to petabytes—using standard SQL(mailnly) or Python.

Core Characteristics -
-> Serverless Architecture: You do not need to provision, manage, or tune underlying virtual machines, servers, or hardware clusters. 
                            Google automatically scales compute and storage resources up or down based on your workload

-> Separation of Compute and Storage: The storage layer (which holds and compresses data) and the compute layer (powered by Google's Dremel query engine) operate independently. 
                                      This allows you to scale them separately without performance bottlenecks.

-> Columnar Storage: Data is stored in columns rather than rows, which dramatically speeds up analytical queries by scanning only the specific columns needed
